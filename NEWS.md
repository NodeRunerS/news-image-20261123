## Lead story

1. **NATO warns Russia could put a nuclear weapon in orbit within weeks**
   — Western intelligence services, including Germany's BND and armed
   forces, believe Russia has built a nuclear-armed satellite named
   Sputnik-S, assembled years ago and stored in the Moscow region, and
   could launch it as early as December 2026. A nuclear blast in orbit
   could disable about a third of low-Earth-orbit satellites within hours
   or days, US Space Command chief Gen. Stephen Whiting warned. The
   October US Defense Intelligence Agency assessment also found Russia
   developing a nuclear-capable satellite; deploying one would breach the
   1967 Outer Space Treaty. Russia's embassy rejected the claims as
   "propaganda". Sources: Reuters ("NATO believes Russia is ready to
   launch nuclear-armed satellite, German media say", 23 Nov 2026);
   WDR / NDR / Süddeutsche Zeitung joint investigation; Tagesschau;
   Ukrainska Pravda; United24 Media.

5. **Huang's affair linked to a Chinese spy, NYT says** — Alice Huang
   was in touch with a Chinese spy-agency (MSS) officer during her affair
   with former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, the New York Times reported; US
   intelligence tracked the contacts. Trump again backed Huang, saying
   Nvidia should rehire him as CEO. Sources: New York Times, via The
   Economic Times (23 Nov 2026) and Times Now (23 Nov 2026).

6. **Candy factory fire: 24 dead, ~30 still missing** — Indonesia
   confirmed 24 deaths in the fire at the Fonusa Agung Mulia candy
   factory in Cikande, Banten; about 30 workers are still missing, 29
   are confirmed safe (5 in hospital), and DNA sampling is under way
   to identify the victims. The fire broke out on Saturday evening,
   possibly from an air-conditioner short circuit. Sources: Reuters
   (23 Nov 2026); BusinessWorld (23 Nov 2026).

7. **After the collapse, climate talks move to Ethiopia** — COP31 in
   Antalya ended on Sunday 22 November with no overarching deal after
   two days of overtime, the longest COP on record and the first summit
   failure since Copenhagen in 2009. The EU, UK and island states
   rejected the Antalya Pact because it named no fossil fuels and dropped
   the $120bn adaptation target. COP32 goes to Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.
   Sources: CNN (22 Nov 2026); Al Jazeera (22 Nov 2026); Firstpost
   (23 Nov 2026); The Daily Star (23 Nov 2026).

8. **Bitcoin rebounds above $61,000 after the $55k flush** — Bitcoin
   climbed back to about $61,513 on Monday, up roughly 11% from its
   weekend low near $55,110, the lowest since September 2024. Wall
   Street enters correction after its worst week in 18 months: the S&P
   500 fell 8.6% on the week, the Nasdaq 10%. Sources: KuCoin daily
   market report (23 Nov 2026, 00:00 UTC); Bitget UEX Daily (23 Nov
   2026); AP via Mobee (23 Nov 2026).
